Samarkand

Crossroads of Sogdiana & Jewel of Transoxiana

"Samarkand is the grandest and most splendid city in the world..." — Ibn Battuta

Historical Significance

Located in the fertile Zarafshan River valley, Samarkand was the key metropolis of the Sogdian merchants who brokered trade between China, Persia, and the Mediterranean.

Key Exports / Goods

Raw Silk, Sogdian Glass, Fergana Horses, Paper, Lapis Lazuli

Tech & Culture Transfer

Papermaking Mills, Islamic Astronomy, Zoroastrianism, Buddhism

Era Context: Pax Mongolica

Under Mongol governance, Samarkand thrived along protected imperial post routes (Yam system), welcoming diplomats and traders across Eurasia.
