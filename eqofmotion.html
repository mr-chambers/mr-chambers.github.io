Equations of Motion

We have seen that, given the position function for an object in motion, s (t), we can find the velocity function, v(t), by taking the derivative of s and can find the acceleration function by taking the derivative of v. Instead, suppose we are given the acceleration function and the initial velocity and position. Using antiderivatives we can then find v and s. Let a(t) be the acceleration function, v0 be the initial velocity and s0 be the initial position. Solving the differential equation will give us v(t) (where the constant of integration, C, is v0 in this case). Once we have v, we can then solve to find the position function (where the constant of integration, C, is s0 in this case).
