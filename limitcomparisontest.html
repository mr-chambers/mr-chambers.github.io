Limit Comparison Test

The limit comparison test is similar to the comparison test in that you use another series to show the convergence or divergence of a desired series. Suppose we have two series and , where an >0 and bn > 0. If (i.e., if the ratio of the terms tends to a finite number as n goes to infinity), then both series converge or both series diverge. By picking a suitable B, usually a p-series, we can use this test to determine whether or not A converges.
